# Changelog

## [0.3.0] — 2026-09-05

- replaced weighted Field Constant aggregation with Rosetta 2.0 RCD;
- represented Reciprocity, Embodiment, and Non-Domination as constitutive roles;
- represented Emergence as a downstream role;
- exposed raw and relationally qualified emergence separately;
- changed the operational threshold to the declared product-scale starting point `0.343`; and
- added non-compensation and downstream-emergence tests.

The threshold is an operational research default, not a validated universal
boundary. The lexical evaluator remains a heuristic reference implementation.
